Goals

A deadline heats up as it nears and keeps counting after you miss it. Something good glows as it nears, celebrates, and stops. No time given means the end of that day. Typing instead? 10m is ten minutes; months need mo.

Point a phone at it

Whoever scans this gets the whole board on their own screen — the goals, the dates, all of it. Useful on a projector or a shared monitor, stuck to a wall or a site hoarding, or printed on the front of a plan.

Baaki बाकी

A date you keep not looking at is a date you miss. Put one number where you already look, and you stop needing to be reminded.

How

  1. Add a goal. Press G and type when — friday 6pm, 31 mar 2027, in 3 weeks.
  2. Leave it somewhere you pass. A pinned tab, a spare monitor, a phone on a desk. Press F for fullscreen — any old laptop becomes a wall board.
  3. Send it. The link is the whole countdown, so pasting it into a chat sends it working. Q puts it on a phone from across the room.

Why one number

A number asks nothing of you. Walk past it every day, for months, and it still works.

Inspired by E. Sreedharan. He ran the Delhi Metro on a board in every room, showing days left and nothing else. It finished early. Worth ten minutes.

बाकी is Hindi for what's remaining. No server, no account — just target minus now.
