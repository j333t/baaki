Goals

A deadline heats up as it nears and keeps counting after you miss it. Something good glows as it nears, celebrates, and stops. No time given means the end of that day. Typing instead? 10m is ten minutes; months need mo.

Point a phone at it

Whoever scans this gets the whole board on their own screen — the goals, the dates, all of it. Useful on a projector or a shared monitor, stuck to a wall or a site hoarding, or printed on the front of a plan.

Baaki बाकी

A date you keep not looking at is a date you miss. Put one number where you already look, and you stop needing to be reminded.

How

  1. Add a goal. Press G and type when — friday 6pm, 31 mar 2027, in 3 weeks.
  2. Leave it somewhere you pass. A pinned tab, a spare monitor, a phone on a desk. Press F and any old laptop is a wall board.
  3. Send it. The whole countdown is in the link, so paste it into any chat and it arrives working. Q puts it on somebody's phone from across a room.

Why it is only one number

A chart asks to be studied. A list asks to be triaged. A single number asks nothing, which is why you can put it somewhere you walk past and it still works on the four-hundredth day.

In the offices of the Delhi Metro, E. Sreedharan hung a board in every room showing the days left. Not a plan. Not a chart. One number that everybody walked past — and the Metro finished early and under budget, which almost never happens anywhere. He is worth ten minutes of reading.

बाकी is Hindi for what's remaining. There is no server and no account: a deadline has no state, it is just target minus now.
