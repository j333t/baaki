Baaki बाकी

Baaki is Hindi for what's remaining. It is the only word this tool needs, because it is the only thing on the screen.

In the offices of the Delhi Metro, E. Sreedharan — India's Metro Man — hung a board in every room showing the days left to the next deadline. Not a plan. Not a chart. One number, on a wall, that everybody walked past. The Delhi Metro was finished ahead of schedule and under budget, which almost never happens anywhere. Read about him — he is worth the ten minutes.

This is that board, small enough to email. The goal lives in the link, so sending the link sends the countdown. No server, no account, nothing to sign up for.

Kept on this device, never in the link. How big the number is depends on the room you are standing in, not on the goal.
